3D-PVE Patent Valuation Engine

Turns raw patent records into IP intelligence. It scores each patent on three independent axes (legal, technical and market) using live EPO PATSTAT data, then renders the portfolio as an interactive 3D landscape.

  • SQL pipeline over PATSTAT on BigQuery: applications, citations, IPC codes and legal events.
  • Technical influence measured as the Shannon entropy of cross-domain citations.
  • An ML layer adjusts the composite score for sector risk and market volatility.
  • Live, snapshot and mock data modes, so the demo never breaks on stage.

Helios Protocol solar → AI compute

At noon, home solar panels make far more power than a house uses, and the surplus is often wasted. Helios notices when the home battery is full and puts that surplus energy to work training AI models.

  • FastAPI energy-logic service simulates solar input, battery state and the “duck curve”.
  • When the battery hits 100%, a PyTorch node starts real gradient-descent training as useful proof-of-work.
  • React dashboard with a time controller to scrub through a full day of energy flow.

Information VisualizationResearch · Frontend

PhyloProbe temporal hierarchy engine

Most tree diagrams only show structure. PhyloProbe also shows how each node's health changes over time, so analysts can spot decay, growth and bottlenecks in hierarchies like org charts, budgets and microservice systems.

  • Built on two InfoVis papers: Contact Trees (Khulusi et al., 2016) and TreeVersity (Gomez et al., 2012).
  • A time slider scrubs 20 intervals; each node carries a 5×5 “internal DNA” matrix and sparklines.
  • Three ready-made datasets to explore: social network, finance budget, and microservice health.
